# Data license

The datasets released under this project (DOI-pair tables, typed-diff parquet files, gold-labeled subsets, viewer artifacts) are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC-BY-4.0).

See the full license at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Each release carries a Zenodo DOI. Attribution to the Silent Retraction Corpus and the release DOI is required for downstream use.

The data license applies to the corpus (match tables, diff tables, labels, and derived summaries). It does not and cannot override the licenses of the original scientific papers whose diffs the corpus records. Original papers remain under the terms their publishers set. The corpus records diffs; it does not redistribute full paper text.
